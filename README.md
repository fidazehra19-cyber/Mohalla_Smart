# 🌱 MOHALLA SMART

### AI-Assisted Community Utility and Drainage Alert System

**Original Concept:** Fida Zehra  
**Team:** Green Ninjas  
**Achievement:** 3rd Place in a Poster Making Competition  
**Project Status:** Concept Stage

## 💡 The Problem

Communities may face three common problems:

- ⚡ Unpredictable electricity disruptions
- 💧 Uncertain water supply timings
- 🌧️ Overflowing drains during heavy rain

Information about these issues may not reach all residents at the right time.

## 🚀 Our Proposed Solution

MOHALLA SMART proposes a community information system with two communication channels:

**1. Online Community Board**
- Residents can check utility updates and community notices using their phones.
- Authorized community representatives can share updates.

**2. Offline Physical Notice Board**
- Residents without internet access can check the same important notices.
- Information remains accessible without depending entirely on smartphones.

## 🤖 Future Scope: AI and Sensors

- Drainage sensors could detect rising water levels.
- AI could analyse historical disruption data to estimate possible interruptions.
- Timely alerts could help residents prepare for potential problems.

*These are proposed future features; the system has not yet been implemented.*

## 🌍 Sustainability and Social Impact

- Supports inclusive access to community information.
- Encourages better communication and community participation.
- Could help reduce avoidable inconvenience through timely updates.
- Aligns with relevant Sustainable Development Goals, including SDG 6, SDG 9, SDG 11 and SDG 16.

The next stage could involve designing a simple digital prototype, testing the community update workflow, and exploring affordable sensor integration.
